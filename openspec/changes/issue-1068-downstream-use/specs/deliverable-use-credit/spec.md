## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Deliverable use requires independently checkable evidence
A textual fact reference SHALL remain auditable but SHALL NOT establish downstream use. Full deliverable credit SHALL require a checker attestation identifying the fact, the exact deliverable and fact content, and an independently checkable shared substantive excerpt. The checker SHALL be distinct from the fact producer. Unknown or invalid evidence SHALL remain uncredited.

#### Scenario: Incidental mention
- **WHEN** a verified fact is mentioned without qualifying use evidence
- **THEN** its credit is identical to the no-mention case

#### Scenario: Use established later
- **WHEN** qualifying evidence of downstream use is discovered after settlement
- **THEN** the original producer receives the amendment once and replay does not duplicate mass

#### Scenario: Rule-out
- **WHEN** an independent verified rule-out exists
- **THEN** its existing credit path remains unchanged
