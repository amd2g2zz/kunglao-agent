# Proposal

## Why

A fact identifier in a deliverable does not establish that the fact contributed to the deliverable. Treating every mention as use can award the producer unearned credit.

## What Changes

- Preserve references as audit information, and require independently checkable evidence of use for deliverable-based credit.
- Preserve the question-link and independently verified rule-out paths.
- Exercise initial and delayed settlement, duplicate replay, and producer posterior effects with a deterministic fixture.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `deliverable-use-credit`: Credit facts for deliverable use only when supported by verifiable downstream evidence.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Round-credit artifact extraction and settlement, tests, and documentation. Legacy textual references remain readable but do not independently establish use.
