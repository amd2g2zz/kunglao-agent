## Design

The textual citation flag remains audit data; it does not itself qualify for credit. A checker records an explicit `deliverable-use/1` result identifying the fact and deliverable, binding both full-file SHA-256 values, an exact substantive excerpt and an explanation of its contribution. Fact and deliverable bytes must both still match, and the checker identity must differ from known producer identities. The checker judges the contribution in context; a byte match is a necessary check, not sufficient evidence alone.

The workspace checker identity follows the existing independent rule-out signoff model. It is not authenticated against the host session; #1055 is separate and explicitly deferred. Unknown, absent, invalid, and legacy references do not receive deliverable credit. Existing question-link and independently verified rule-out semantics are unchanged. Existing settlement reconciliation handles later qualifying use without adding duplicate mass.

## Evidence boundaries

The deterministic synthetic fixture exercises the production settlement entry and posterior with a fixed question link. It does not measure real task success or dispatch improvement.
