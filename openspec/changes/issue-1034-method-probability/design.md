## Known limits (2026-11-23)

- The production stamp (`_stamp_ope_propensity` → `method_decision`) labels no information set: a stamped receipt carries no `information_set` field. A design whose receipt expects `information_set: None` therefore passes the evaluator's information-set gate for a transition that names no information set either — for such a design the gate holds without content. The stamping-path regression in `tests/test_ope_randomized_1034.py` relies on this.
- The preregistered tolerance 4 × sqrt(sum p(1−p)) + 1 uses the variance conditional on each draw's posterior sample, and the count's marginal spread over fresh seeds is larger. Computed from the law's draws: asymmetric 25.51 against 22.59 actions (×1.13), history 28.63 against 25.74 (×1.11), downweighted 33.92 against 31.84 (×1.07). The tolerance therefore sits at 3.54, 3.60 and 3.75 marginal standard deviations instead of 4; under a normal approximation that refuses a correct sampler in about 4.0, 3.2 and 1.7 runs per 10,000 per configuration, against 0.63 at the nominal 4. The tolerance is left as preregistered and must be corrected before the calibration is used as a gate.
