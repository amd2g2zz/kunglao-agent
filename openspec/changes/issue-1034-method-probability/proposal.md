## Why

Production transitions can carry a scalar corroborated by a recipe sampler. A method-family evaluation that joins it to a family receipt by equal number and claim can misattribute a recipe decision to a method decision. A claim can be dispatched multiple times.

## What Changes

Bind method-family evaluation to an identified execution and its probability information set. Exclude historical, recipe-only and otherwise unbound carriers from method OPE with a counted reason. Retain recipe sampling records and the controlled-experiment population and overlap checks.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `method-family-ope`: admission of transition evidence into method-family OPE.

## Impact

The method-family audit/transition join in the offline evaluator and its controlled experiment fixture. No change to online learning, recipe selection, existing persisted records or base model.
