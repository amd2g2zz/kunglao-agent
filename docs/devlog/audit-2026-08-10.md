## Classification Logic

### BLIND Signature (Issue #16)

Each PROVEN claim in `claim-register.yaml` is cross-referenced against `facts/_INDEX.md`:

- **verified**: at least one fact entry has `BLIND` in its status column (e.g. `VERIFIED-BY-W01 (kong-redteam BLIND)`).
- **has-evidence-no-signoff**: facts show `VERIFIED-BY-*` (a verifier subagent ran), but no `BLIND` independent signoff.
- **unverified**: facts are plain `PROVEN` with no verifier trace whatsoever.

### Index Traceability (Issue #26)

Each PROVEN claim's fact files are checked for provenance blocks that cite paths registered in `evidence/_index.json` (built in-memory by P1 `build_evidence_index`). For each provenance ref, the path is looked up in the index and the file's sha256 is compared against the index entry's sha256:

- **has-raw-evidence**: at least one provenance ref resolves to an index entry with matching sha256. The claim is traceable to raw evidence.
- **derivation-only**: fact has provenance refs but none resolve to indexed raw evidence (all cite derived files, unindexed directories, or hash mismatches).
- **unverifiable**: fact file missing, no provenance block, or no evidence index can be built.

## Findings

1. **98% false-PROVEN rate confirmed**: 46/47 PROVEN claims lack BLIND verification, matching the PRD's measured baseline.
2. **Two-tier problem**: 22 claims have zero verifier activity (unverified), while 24 have partial verifier work that stopped short of BLIND (has-evidence-no-signoff). The latter group may need less effort to promote to BLIND-verified.
3. **Single BLIND-verified claim** (C-401) was verified in the most recent campaign (2026-08-05), confirming the BLIND mechanism works when enforced — it just wasn't enforced historically.
4. **STAMP claim F062/C-402** correctly excluded — it is `STAMP-BY-W02 (verifier pending)`, not PROVEN.
5. **NEGATIVE facts (F027, F042)** correctly excluded from PROVEN audit scope.
6. **Index traceability: 21.3%** — only 10/47 PROVEN claims have fact provenance that resolves to raw evidence via the index. 18 claims cite only unindexed or derived paths (many cite `fixtures/`, `decomp/`, `bins/` which are outside the index scan scope of `evidence/` + `analysis_artifacts/`). 19 claims have no fact linked in _INDEX.md at all.
7. **Traceability gap correlates with BLIND gap**: the 10 has-raw-evidence claims overlap heavily with the has-evidence-no-signoff BLIND category — these are the claims closest to being fully re-verifiable via index-traced raw evidence.
